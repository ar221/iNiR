## 1. Dream Outcome

You sit down to write something — a brief, a summary, a decision — and the thing you half-remember saving eight months ago is *right there*. You ask a plain question: "What did I save about X?" You get an answer built from your own notes and files, with a line showing exactly which document it came from. No more opening twelve apps. No more "I know I saved this somewhere." No more re-finding what you already found once.

The end state is simple: everything you've ever saved becomes one place you can ask questions of, and trust the answer because it traces back to your own material. Your saved chaos stops being a graveyard and starts being a tool you actually use.

## 2. Perceived Likelihood of Achievement

This works because of what it *isn't*. It isn't a generic AI guessing from the open web. It's grounded in **your own files** — every answer is built from material you saved, and every generated brief carries a trail back to the source document. If it can't find it in your stuff, it doesn't make it up.

It stays **local-first**: your material lives on your machine, under your control. Nothing gets uploaded to a platform you don't own. The intelligence layer reads relevant excerpts to answer a question; the vault itself is yours.

It's **human-guided**. I do the audit and the setup with you in the room, so the structure fits how *you* actually think, not a template.

And the proof that matters most: **I run this exact system on my own life, every day.** This isn't a product I'm theorizing about. It's the thing I built for myself, use daily, and am now setting up for other people. You're buying a system that already survived contact with a real, messy, over-saved life — mine.

## 3. Time Delay

You feel the first win inside one session. The pilot is a single **1–2 hour** sitting: we go through your current mess, design a simple structure, set up capture and source tracking, and produce one real output from your own material before we're done. You leave that session with something working, not a roadmap.

After setup, the speed is the point: you ask, you get an answer — grounded in your files, with the source attached. The slow part (the audit and the wiring) is the part *I* do. The fast part (asking and getting answers) is the part you keep.

## 4. Effort & Sacrifice

You do almost nothing. I do the audit, the structure, the wiring, and the first output. You bring your material; I make it usable.

- **No migration homework.** You don't reorganize anything before we start. The mess *is* the starting material.
- **No new app to learn.** This sits on top of how you already work. You keep your existing tools and files.
- **Nothing leaves your control.** It's local-first — your data stays on your machine, not in someone else's cloud.
- **No commitment to a workflow you'll abandon.** The capture inbox means new stuff has a home automatically; you're not signing up to maintain a complicated system by hand.

The only thing you have to do is show up to the session with your saved material. I handle the rest.
